The Story of Drinaturals

From a kitchen experiment to your family's favourite snack

The First Batch

It all started with a simple idea — why not dehydrate the freshest fruits and make them available as a clean, healthy snack for everyone. Hand-selected, single-ingredient, and nothing else.

Market Launch

From local pop-ups and farmers' markets to being stocked at specialty grocers like Le Marché in Islamabad and Shams. Drinaturals quickly became a favourite among health-conscious families.

Growing Family

With demand growing, Drinaturals expanded its range — from strawberries and apples to mangoes, bananas, citrus slices, and mixed fruit packs. Available direct-to-consumer via Instagram and at select retailers.
